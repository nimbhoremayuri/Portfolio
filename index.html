Zepto My Café | Case Study Overview

Executive Summary

Zepto Café delivers freshly prepared beverages and snacks in 10 minutes. However, power users who order daily coffee or evening snacks face repetitive browsing friction. "Zepto My Café" introduces a personalized quick-reorder hub, custom combo builder, and streak-based loyalty experience.

1. User Pain Points Discovered
  • • Too many taps required to customize sugar/milk preferences every morning.
  • • Difficulty pairing hot beverages with snack items for free-delivery thresholds.
  • • Lack of habit-forming rewards for consecutive weekday café orders.
2. Key Product Solutions
  • ✓ Saved Taste Profiles: One-tap reorder with saved customization presets.
  • ✓ Smart Combo Bundling: Dynamic snack pairings priced to hit optimal AOV.
  • ✓ 5-Cup Streak Pass: Unlock a complimentary beverage on the 5th order.
3. North Star & Guardrail Metrics
North Star
Weekly Café Orders / User
Primary KPI
1-Tap Reorder Adoption %
Guardrail
Prep-to-Dispatch SLA (<10m)
